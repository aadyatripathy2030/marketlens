ChartGauge pulls the price history, computes SMA 20/50/200, RSI, MACD, Bollinger bands, ATR, VWAP, support and resistance, draws them on a candlestick chart, and writes up what they say. It is a study tool. The indicators disagree with each other often, and the score built from them is wrong often.

      What’s moving right now

      The most active US stocks, ranked by how unusual the activity is: volume against the symbol’s own normal, the size of today’s move, and how wide the day has been. Every number here describes what has happened. None of it forecasts the next five minutes.

      Unusual volume tells you people are trading it, not which way it goes next. ChartGauge’s own score was measured against 36,524 past setups and did not predict direction — every symbol below links to its chart, where the measured base rate for that setup is shown honestly. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell.
