      How accurate is ChartGauge?

      Every reading this site shows is written down when it is shown — before anyone knows what happens next — and graded against what the price actually did afterwards. The record below is published whether it flatters the tool or not.

      This is not a backtest. A backtest can be adjusted until the past looks good; these are timestamped readings recorded in advance and never revised. The comparison column is every graded reading taken together — the return you would have had without consulting the score at all.

      Why publish this? Because the alternative is asking you to take it on faith, and the measured answer so far is that this score has not predicted direction. A tool that tells you when it does not work is more useful than one that never admits it.
